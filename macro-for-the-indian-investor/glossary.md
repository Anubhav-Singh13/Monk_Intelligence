# Glossary

Plain-English first, formal second, then the day the term is introduced. Terms are alphabetical. If a term feels fuzzy on a later page, this is your fastest lookup.

| Term | Plain English | Formal | Introduced |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Anchoring** | Your guess sticks near the first number you saw. | A cognitive bias where estimates are pulled toward an initial reference value. | Day 18 |
| **Basis point (bp)** | One-hundredth of a percent; 25 bp = 0.25%. | 1 bp = 0.01 percentage points. | Day 10 |
| **Business cycle** | The economy's short-run ups and downs around its trend. | Recurring expansions and contractions in output relative to potential. | Day 2 |
| **Core inflation** | Inflation with the jumpy bits (food, fuel) stripped out. | Price change excluding volatile food and energy components. | Day 9 |
| **CPI (Consumer Price Index)** | The cost of a typical household's shopping basket. | Weighted average price of a fixed consumption basket; India's headline and policy target. | Day 9 |
| **Credit spread** | Extra yield a risky borrower pays over the government. | Yield difference between a corporate bond and a same-maturity G-sec. | Day 20 |
| **Current Account Deficit (CAD)** | India spends more abroad than it earns; the gap. | Excess of imports + net outflows over exports + net inflows, as % of GDP. | Day 19 |
| **Discount rate** | The interest rate you use to value future money today. | The rate at which future cash flows are converted to present value. | Day 17 |
| **Dual mandate / flexible inflation targeting** | Hit an inflation target without ignoring growth. | A regime targeting CPI (4% ±2% in India) while considering output. | Day 13 |
| **Equity risk premium (ERP)** | Extra return stocks must offer over bonds to be worth the risk. | Expected equity return minus the risk-free rate. | Day 17 |
| **Expectations** | What the market already believes will happen. | The consensus forecast embedded in current prices. | Day 3 |
| **FII / FPI flows** | Foreign money moving into or out of Indian assets. | Net purchases by Foreign Institutional / Portfolio Investors. | Day 19 |
| **Fiscal deficit** | The government spends more than it earns; it borrows the rest. | Government expenditure minus revenue, as % of GDP. | Day 16 |
| **Fiscal policy** | The government's spending-and-tax lever. | Use of government budget to influence aggregate demand. | Day 5 |
| **GDP** | The total value of everything an economy produces. | Market value of all final goods and services in a period. | Day 6 |
| **GVA (Gross Value Added)** | GDP measured from the production side, before taxes. | GDP minus net indirect taxes; output by sector. | Day 6 |
| **G-sec** | A loan you make to the Government of India. | A rupee-denominated Government of India dated security. | Day 10 |
| **Herding** | Everyone copies everyone; the crowd moves as one. | Correlated behaviour driven by imitation rather than independent information. | Day 18 |
| **IIP (Index of Industrial Production)** | A monthly gauge of factory/mining/utility output. | Volume index of industrial-sector production. | Day 8 |
| **Inflation** | Money buys less over time. | Sustained rise in the general price level. | Day 4 |
| **Monetary policy** | The RBI's interest-rate lever. | Central-bank management of money and interest rates. | Day 5 |
| **MPC (Monetary Policy Committee)** | The six-person RBI committee that sets the repo rate. | Statutory committee setting India's policy rate to meet the CPI target. | Day 13 |
| **Nominal vs. real** | Nominal is the rupee number; real strips out inflation. | Real = nominal adjusted for price change (via a deflator). | Day 6 |
| **Output gap** | How far the economy is running above/below its sustainable level. | Actual output minus potential output, as % of potential. | Day 13 |
| **PCE** | The US's preferred inflation measure (context for the Fed). | Personal Consumption Expenditures price index. | Day 9 |
| **PMI (Purchasing Managers' Index)** | A survey; above 50 = expansion, below = contraction. | Diffusion index of business activity from purchasing managers. | Day 8 |
| **Policy rate / repo rate** | The RBI's key interest rate — the anchor price of money. | Rate at which the RBI lends overnight to banks against collateral. | Day 10 |
| **Prospect theory** | Losses hurt about twice as much as equal gains feel good. | A model of choice with a reference-dependent, loss-averse value function. | Day 18 |
| **Reflexivity** | Prices change fundamentals, which change prices — a loop. | Feedback in which market prices influence the reality they reference. | Day 25 |
| **Regime** | The prevailing growth-and-inflation weather. | A persistent macro state (e.g., growth-up/inflation-down) shaping asset returns. | Day 21 |
| **Risk premium** | Extra return demanded for bearing uncertainty. | Expected excess return over the risk-free rate for a risky asset. | Day 17 |
| **Stance** | The RBI's signalled direction: accommodative / neutral / withdrawal. | Forward guidance on the likely path of policy. | Day 23 |
| **Surprise** | The gap between what happened and what was expected. | Actual minus consensus; the component that moves price. | Day 3 |
| **Transmission** | How a repo change actually reaches loans and asset prices. | The channels (rates, credit, exchange rate, expectations) linking policy to the economy. | Day 14 |
| **Taylor rule** | A formula for where the policy rate "should" be. | Policy rate = f(inflation gap, output gap). | Day 13 |
| **WPI (Wholesale Price Index)** | Prices at the factory/wholesale gate, not the shop. | Price index of goods traded in bulk; not India's policy target. | Day 9 |
| **Yield curve** | Interest rates plotted from short to long maturities. | The term structure of G-sec yields across maturities. | Day 11 |

*Glossary grows with the course. If you meet a bolded term on a page that isn't here yet, it will be added on the day it's introduced.*
