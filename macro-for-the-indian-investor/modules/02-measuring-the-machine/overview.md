# Module 2 — Measuring the Machine (Days 6–12)

> **What this module earns you:** the ability to read the actual numbers India publishes — GDP/GVA, the high-frequency activity data, CPI, the repo rate, and the G-sec yield curve — and know what each one is really telling you, not just its headline.

Module 1 gave you the machine as a picture. Now you learn to read its gauges. You'll dissect a GDP print into the components that matter to an investor (Days 6–7), learn what India watches *between* quarterly prints because it has no monthly payrolls (Day 8), take inflation apart into CPI vs. WPI and understand why food and fuel make India's number behave differently (Day 9), meet the single most important price in finance — the repo rate — and the G-secs it anchors (Day 10), and read the yield curve as the bond market's forecast (Day 11). Day 12 is a Rest & Synthesize checkpoint: you reconstruct the whole measurement arc from memory before the policy machinery arrives.

**The pages**

**Bottleneck note:** Day 10 (the repo rate as *the* anchor) and Day 11 (the curve as a forecast) are the pivots from "macro" to "your portfolio." If either is shaky, the transmission drills in Module 3 will not land.
