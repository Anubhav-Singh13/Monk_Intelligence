# Module 1 — Foundations (Days 1–5)

> **What this module earns you:** a working mental model of the economy as a *machine* — a loop of spending, growing along a trend but wobbling in a cycle, watched by markets that trade the *future*, and steered by two hands. No equations yet. Just the pictures everything else will hang on.

By the end of Day 5 you will be able to explain, over coffee, why one person's spending is another's income, why "growth" and "the cycle" are different questions, why the Nifty can fall on good news, what inflation actually *is*, and who the two players are whose announcements move your portfolio. These five intuitions are the load-bearing walls; Modules 2–5 are the rooms built on top.

**The five ideas**

| Day | Idea | The picture |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The economy is just transactions | A closed loop where every rupee spent is income to someone else. |
| 2 | Trend vs. the wobble | A rising escalator (growth) that you also jog up and down on (the cycle). |
| 3 | Markets price the future | Prices already hold the expected news; only the *surprise* moves them. |
| 4 | Inflation is money vs. goods | Too much spending chasing too few goods erodes the rupee. |
| 5 | Who steers | The RBI (rates) and the Government (budget) — two hands on one wheel. |

**Bottleneck note:** the sub-skill this whole course drills — chaining a macro event to an asset move — begins here in embryo on Day 3. Give that page extra attention; everything downstream leans on it.
