# Module 3 — How Policy Moves Markets (Days 13–16)

> **What this module earns you:** the mechanism at the heart of the whole course — how the RBI decides, how a repo change actually reaches your loans and your portfolio (and why it does so slowly in India), and how the Government's borrowing feeds into the same bond market. Then your first real drill.

You can now read the gauges. This module shows you the hands on the controls. Day 13 makes RBI decisions *predictable* by giving you its reaction function (the Taylor-rule intuition and flexible inflation targeting). Day 14 is the beating heart: the transmission mechanism — repo → bank rates, discount rates, the rupee, credit — and the very Indian problem of *why transmission is leaky*. Day 15 is **Drill I**, where you stop reading and start doing: seven isolated reps chaining a single shock to bonds, equities, the rupee, and credit spreads. Day 16 adds the second lever — fiscal policy, the Union Budget, and the deficit that the bond market has to absorb.

**The pages**

| Day | Idea |
|---|---|
| 13 | The MPC moves the repo in response to inflation and growth. |
| 14 | Transmission — the mechanism, and why India's is slow. |
| 15 | **Drill I** — chain a shock to Nifty / G-sec / INR / credit. |
| 16 | Deficits get financed, so bond markets price government borrowing. |

**Bottleneck note:** this *is* the bottleneck module. Day 14 (transmission) and Day 15 (the drill) are where the course either works or doesn't. Do the drill honestly — no peeking at the mechanism while you chain the shock.
