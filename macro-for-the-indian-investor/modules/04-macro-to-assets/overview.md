# Module 4 — From Macro to Assets (Days 17–21)

> **What this module earns you:** the payoff. You take everything from the machine and turn it into a lens for pricing assets — one master equation (the discount rate), the behavioural distortions around it, the rupee as the global variable, credit spreads as the fear gauge, and finally a map from macro regime to what to own.

This is where "understanding why markets move" becomes "understanding what to do about it." Day 17 gives you the master equation: every asset is priced by rates + growth + a risk premium. Day 18 — the behavioural engine — explains why real prices over- and undershoot that equation, because humans anchor and herd. Day 19 adds the rupee, where domestic and global forces (oil, the Fed, FII flows) collide. Day 20 introduces credit spreads, the market's fear gauge that tends to *lead* equities. Day 21 assembles it all into the four-regime map: growth × inflation, and what each quadrant does to Indian asset classes.

**Bottleneck note:** Day 17 (discount-rate lens) is the unifier — it turns Days 3 and 10 into a single tool you'll use for the rest of your investing life. Day 21 is the concrete payoff you were promised on Day 1.
