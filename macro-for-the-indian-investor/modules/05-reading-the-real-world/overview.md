# Module 5 — Reading the Real World (Days 22–27)

> **What this module earns you:** the ability to do this for real. You learn to read a live data release and a live RBI statement the way a market professional does, drill the whole chain adversarially, understand when the model breaks, consolidate everything, and then produce your own macro thesis on genuinely current data.

Everything so far has been building the apparatus. This module points it at the world. Day 22 teaches you to read a data release the way the market does — reacting to the gap between print and consensus, and why good news can be bad news. Day 23 does the same for the RBI's statement, stance, and minutes (with the Fed as overlay). Day 24 is **Drill II**: eight adversarial scenarios where the lazy narrative and the crowd's bias pay you the wrong way. Day 25 is the humility page — regime shifts and reflexive bubbles that defeat every tidy rule. Day 26 is the final Rest & Synthesize. Day 27 is the **capstone**: real, recent Indian data and the latest MPC resolution, turned into a one-page thesis a colleague could act on.

**The pages**

| Day | Idea |
|---|---|
| 22 | Price reacts to print-minus-consensus, not the level. |
| 23 | Read the RBI's statement, stance, and minutes for the *change*. |
| 24 | **Drill II** — mechanism vs. narrative, adversarial. |
| 25 | Regime shifts and reflexive bubbles defeat tidy rules. |
| 26 | **Rest & Synthesize II** — the whole course from memory. |
| 27 | **Capstone** — your own thesis on live data. |

**Bottleneck note:** Day 24 is the final, hardest test of the core sub-skill. If you can pass it cold, you've got what the course promised. Day 27 proves it on data that didn't exist when this course was written.
