# Module 5 — Harness Engineering: Reliability & Operations

**What this module earns you:** the operations half of ④ **Harness → Runtime** and the measurement side of ⑤ **Loop → Feedback** — the difference between a demo and a system you'd put in front of users. It comes now because you can only harden an agent that already loops and manages context.

By the end your agent survives tool and model failures (retries, breakers, idempotency), you can *measure* whether it works (a real eval harness, not vibes — also how you chose your model on Day 3), and you can see and control what it costs (tracing, budgets, caching, routing).
