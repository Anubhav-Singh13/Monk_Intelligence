# Module 4 — Loop Engineering: Control & Orchestration

**What this module earns you:** the control half of ⑤ **Loop → Feedback**, plus the first taste of ⑥ **Graph → Coordination** — making a loop *stop correctly* and deciding when to split one loop into many. These depend on memory (Module 3), which is why they come now.

By the end your loop enforces budgets, detects when it's stuck, verifies completion claims, integrates the whole per-turn pipeline (the big drill), and you can reason about when multi-agent coordination earns its cost.
