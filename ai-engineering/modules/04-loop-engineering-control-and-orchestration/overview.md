# Module 4 — Loop Engineering: Control & Orchestration

**What this module earns you:** the second half of loop engineering — making a loop *stop correctly* and deciding when to split one loop into many. These depend on memory (Module 3), which is why they come now.

By the end your loop enforces budgets, detects when it's stuck, verifies completion claims, integrates the whole per-turn pipeline (the big drill), and you can reason about when multi-agent orchestration earns its cost — and when it doesn't.
