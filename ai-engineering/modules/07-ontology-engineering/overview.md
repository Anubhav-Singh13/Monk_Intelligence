# Module 7 — Ontology Engineering

**What this module earns you:** discipline ⑦ **Ontology → Shared Meaning** — the schema of *meaning* beneath everything else. Taught last because you've now felt fragmented extraction and incoherent queries, but conceptually foundational: your entity definitions constrain prompts, shape context, and populate graphs.

By the end you can design a lightweight ontology (classes, relations, attributes — scoped by competency questions) that gives your system one shared vocabulary, and enforce it with executable business rules and constraints at the write boundary — turning "the model said so" into "this conforms to our rules."
