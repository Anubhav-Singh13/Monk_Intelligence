# Module 0 — Foundations

**What this module earns you:** the mental model the whole course runs on — AI engineering as *systems work around a rented model*, and the model itself as a stateless CPU wrapped by an OS-like harness.

By the end of these two days you can say what distinguishes AI engineering from ML, name its four disciplines (harness, loop, context, graph), and explain why the harness — not the model — is where your leverage lives.
