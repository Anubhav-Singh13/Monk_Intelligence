# Module 4 — Sattvāpatti: The Stabilising (Days 29–39)

Back to [README](../../README.md) · Previous module: [Tanumānasā](../03-tanumanasa/overview.md)

**What this module earns you:** a stable, defensible understanding of *aham brahmāsmi* — one you can reconstruct cold, defend against objections, recognise the obstacles to, and carry into the primary texts on your own.

*Sattvāpatti* ("attainment of clarity") is the fourth bhūmikā: knowledge becomes direct and steady. The module moves in three steps:

1. **Convergence** (Days 29–31): the direct path as a fourth door; the four invariants every source shares; and the mechanism by which knowledge removes ignorance and then itself.
2. **Stress-testing** (Days 32–34): the hardest drill, the named obstacles, and the radical texts as a test of understanding.
3. **Living it and handing off** (Days 36–39): what steadiness looks like, what remains of the personality, a 12-month reading plan, and the capstone.

Day 35 consolidates. Stages 5–7 lie beyond this course; Day 38 points you to where they are described.
