# Module 2 — Vicāraṇā: The Enquiry (Days 6–20)

Back to [README](../../README.md) · Previous module: [Shubheccha](../01-shubheccha/overview.md)

**What this module earns you:** the full conceptual machinery of Advaita, rebuilt from one rule — *whatever is seen is not the seer* — and the ability to run the two great methods (*tat tvam asi* and *neti neti*) yourself.

*Vicāraṇā* is the second bhūmikā: study and enquiry under the teaching. This is the longest module because it carries the argument. It moves in three arcs:

1. **The discrimination** (Days 6–10): seer vs. seen, applied across layers (five sheaths) and across time (three states), then *turīya*.
2. **The diagnosis** (Days 11–13): how words point to Brahman, why bondage is superimposition (*adhyāsa*), and what status the world has (*mithyā*).
3. **The methods** (Days 15–20): māyā and Īśvara, the equation *tat tvam asi*, negation *neti neti*, Gauḍapāda's *ajātivāda*, and the three-step route of hearing–reflecting–dwelling.

Drill days 9 and 19 hit the course's bottleneck skill. Day 14 consolidates.
