# Module 3 — Tanumānasā: The Thinning (Days 21–28)

Back to [README](../../README.md) · Previous module: [Vicāraṇā](../02-vicharana/overview.md)

**What this module earns you:** the practices that quieten the mind enough for the knowledge to hold, and three modern "doors" that turn the classical discrimination into a live practice.

*Tanumānasā* ("thin mind") is the third bhūmikā: as enquiry continues, desires weaken and the mind becomes subtle and transparent. The module has two halves:

1. **Preparation** (Days 21–24): karma yoga, bhakti, meditation — and the precise line between preparing the mind and knowing the Self. Vidyāraṇya's three practices tie them together.
2. **Living doors** (Days 26–28): Ramana's "Who am I?", Nisargadatta's "I Am", Krishnamurti's "the observer is the observed". Each page shows how the method maps back onto Module 2's machinery — they converge on the same discrimination.
