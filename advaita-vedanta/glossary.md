# Glossary

Back to [README](README.md) · [Learning path](learning-path.md) · [Bibliography](bibliography.md)

Alphabetical (diacritics ignored for ordering). Updated as each day is written.

| Term | Plain English | Formal | Introduced |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Abhyāsa** | Practice | Repeated effort; with *vairāgya*, the means of mastering the mind (Gītā 6.35) | Day 23 |
| **Adhikārī** | A student ready for the teaching | One endowed with the *sādhana-catuṣṭaya*, hence fit (*adhikāra*) for Vedānta | Day 3 |
| **Adhiṣṭhāna** | The substrate an error appears on | The real basis (rope) on which the superimposed (snake) appears; ultimately Brahman | Day 12 |
| **Adhyāropa–apavāda** | Say it, then take it back | The teaching method of provisional attribution followed by retraction | Day 17 |
| **Adhyāsa** | Mistaking one thing for another | Superimposition: *smṛti-rūpaḥ paratra pūrva-dṛṣṭāvabhāsaḥ* — appearance, in the form of memory, of something previously seen, on something else (Adhyāsa Bhāṣya) | Day 12 |
| **Āgāmī (kriyamāṇa) karma** | Incoming karma — the arrow on the string | Results of present and future action; does not cling to the knower (BS 4.1.13–14) | Day 37 |
| **Aham-vṛtti** | The "I"-thought | The first modification of the mind, on which all other thoughts depend (Ramana, *Nān Yār?*) | Day 26 |
| **Ahaṅkāra** | The ego, the "I-maker" | The mind's "I"-function that claims ownership ("I am angry"); a seen object, not the *sākṣī* | Day 28 |
| **Ajahal-lakṣaṇā** | Pointing by keeping the whole meaning and adding | Non-exclusive implication ("the red runs" = the red horse) — rejected for *tat tvam asi* | Day 16 |
| **Ajātivāda** | Nothing was ever really born | Gauḍapāda's non-origination: from the *pāramārthika* standpoint no creation, bondage or liberation (MK 2.32, 3.48) | Day 18 |
| **Ajñāna** | Not knowing | The first of the tenth man's seven stages — ignorance of one's own nature (Pañcadaśī ch. 7) | Day 4 |
| **Akhaṇḍākāra vṛtti** | The one thought "I am the limitless" | A mental modification in the form of the undivided, born of understanding the *mahāvākya*; destroys ignorance, then subsides as itself an effect of ignorance (*Vedāntasāra*) | Day 31 |
| **Alātaśānti** | The quenching of the firebrand | MK ch. 4: appearances neither issue from nor return into consciousness, like shapes of a whirled torch | Day 18 |
| **Ānanda** | Fullness felt as happiness | The limitlessness (*ananta*) of the Self, reflected in a quiet mind as joy | Day 1 |
| **Ānandamaya kośa** | The bliss sheath | The sheath "made of bliss" (*priya, moda, pramoda*), most evident in deep sleep; still a sheath, resting on Brahman (Taittirīya 2.5) | Day 7 |
| **Anātman** | No-self | The Buddhist teaching that no self is found among the aggregates; Advaita agrees there is no self "on the list" and differs on the status of awareness | Day 32 |
| **Annamaya kośa** | The body as a sheath | The "food-made" sheath — the physical body (Taittirīya 2.1–2.2) | Day 7 |
| **Anubandha-catuṣṭaya** | A text's four opening credentials | *Adhikārī* (qualified reader), *viṣaya* (subject), *prayojana* (result), *sambandha* (relation of text to subject) | Day 38 |
| **Anubhava** | Immediate knowing / "experience" | Classically, knowledge of Brahman become immediate (BSBh 1.1.2); in modern usage often a special inner state (Halbfass) | Day 30 |
| **Anvaya–vyatireka** | Finding what stays when everything else changes | Co-presence and co-absence reasoning: what is present when Y is absent is not Y — applied to the three states | Day 8 |
| **Aparokṣa jñāna** | Knowing something as immediately present | Knowledge whose object is not remote — e.g. "I am the tenth" | Day 4 |
| **Ārta / Jijñāsu / Arthārthī** | The distressed / the seeker of knowledge / the seeker of gain | The first three of the four kinds of devotee (Gītā 7.16) | Day 22 |
| **Asaṃsakti** | Non-attachment (stage 5) | Fifth Yoga Vasiṣṭha *bhūmikā*: objects no longer catch the mind | Day 38 |
| **Ātman** | The Self — what "I" ultimately refers to | The witnessing consciousness, non-different from Brahman | Day 5 |
| **Ātma-vicāra** | Self-enquiry | Ramana's method: turning attention from thoughts to the "I" to whom they arise | Day 26 |
| **Avadhūta** | One who has "shaken off" everything | The voice of the *Avadhūta Gītā*: free of every identification, rule and role; a portrait of the result, not a method | Day 34 |
| **Āvaraṇa** | Concealment | What ignorance does first: "it isn't here" — the tenth man is taken as absent | Day 4 |
| **Avasthā-traya** | The three states | Waking (*jāgrat*), dream (*svapna*), deep sleep (*suṣupti*) — examined to separate awareness from its changing contents | Day 8 |
| **Avidyā** | Not-knowing one's own nature | Ignorance that conceals the Self and projects a limited identity | Day 4 |
| **Bādha** | Correction by a truer knowledge | Sublation: an appearance's status is cancelled when its substrate is known; the appearance need not vanish | Day 13 |
| **Bādhitānuvṛtti** | An appearance continuing after it is known to be false | Continuation of the sublated (mirage, sunrise); why the personality persists after knowledge | Day 37 |
| **Bhāga-tyāga lakṣaṇā** | Drop part, keep part | Implication that abandons incompatible parts of two meanings and keeps the common referent (= *jahad-ajahal-lakṣaṇā*); *so 'yaṃ devadattaḥ* | Day 16 |
| **Bhakti** | Devotion | Love directed to Īśvara, the total; purifies the mind and matures into seeing non-difference | Day 22 |
| **Bhāṣya** | Commentary | A classical commentary explaining a root text word by word and answering objectors | Day 38 |
| **Bhūmā** | The infinite / the whole | Chāndogya 7.23: "the infinite alone is happiness; there is no happiness in the small (*alpa*)" | Day 1 |
| **Bhūmikā** | Stage | One of the seven stages of knowledge in the Yoga Vasiṣṭha | README |
| **Brahman** | The limitless reality | That which is not limited by space, time or object (*satyaṃ jñānam anantam*) | Day 1 |
| **Brāhmī sthiti** | The Brahman-state | Settled knowledge from which one is "not deluded again" (Gītā 2.72) | Day 36 |
| **Choiceless awareness** | Seeing without verdict | Krishnamurti: attention that neither condemns, justifies nor identifies, so the observer/observed division ends | Day 28 |
| **Cid-jaḍa-granthi** | The knot of the sentient and the insentient | The ego binding awareness (*cit*) to the inert body-mind (*jaḍa*) — mutual *adhyāsa* (*Ulladu Nārpadu* v. 24) | Day 26 |
| **Cit** | Consciousness itself | Knowing as such — the nature, not an attribute, of Brahman (*jñānam* in Taitt. 2.1) | Day 11 |
| **Citta-śuddhi** | Purification of mind | Thinning of *rāga-dveṣa* that makes the mind fit to hold knowledge (Gītā 5.11) | Day 21 |
| **Cosmological (progressive) path** | Starting from the world | Atmananda: accept the world, trace it to its cause, and come gradually to the Self | Day 29 |
| **Dama** | Restraint of the senses | Withdrawing the organs of action and perception from what is contrary to the goal | Day 3 |
| **Dhīra** | The wise, steady person | One who examines (*samparītya*) śreyas and preyas and chooses śreyas (Kaṭha 1.2.2) | Day 2 |
| **Dhyāna** | Meditation | Steadying the mind on the Self (Gītā ch. 6); preparation for and field of *nididhyāsana* | Day 23 |
| **Direct path** | Starting from present experience | Atmananda's method: examine perception until experience is found to be made only of knowing; the witness taken up, then dropped | Day 29 |
| **Dṛg-dṛśya-viveka** | Telling the seer from the seen | The discrimination that whatever is known is other than the knower; the course's rate-limiting skill | Day 6 |
| **Dṛk** | The seer | That which is aware; in the final sense the witness, never itself seen | Day 6 |
| **Dṛśya** | The seen | Anything that is an object of awareness: forms, sense organs, mind and its modifications | Day 6 |
| **Dvaita** | Dualism | Madhva's school: jīva and Īśvara (and matter) eternally distinct; liberation as service, not identity | Day 32 |
| **Eka-bhakti** | Devotion to the One | The knower's devotion — single because no other object of devotion is seen (Gītā 7.17) | Day 22 |
| **Experience-chasing** | "I had it and lost it" | Mistaking knowledge for a state; a modern form of *saṃśaya* | Day 33 |
| **Hard problem** | Why anything is experienced at all | How physical processes give rise to being-aware; open for physicalism | Day 32 |
| **"I am" / "I am this"** | The bare sense of being / identification | Nisargadatta: being before any predicate (the *sat* pointer) vs. being taken as body, mind or role (*adhyāsa*) | Day 27 |
| **Īśvara** | The Lord — the intelligence of the whole | Brahman associated with *māyā*; the total (*samaṣṭi*), material and intelligent cause of the world, not deceived by it | Day 15 |
| **Īśvara-arpaṇa-buddhi** | The attitude of offering | Action as a contribution to the total (Īśvara), not for the self's standing (Gītā 3.9) | Day 21 |
| **Itaretarādhyāsa** | Two-way mix-up of self and not-self | Mutual superimposition: body–mind features put on the Self, the Self's sentience put on body–mind | Day 12 |
| **Jāgrat** | Waking | The state of outward cognition of the gross world (Māṇḍūkya 3) | Day 8 |
| **Jahal-lakṣaṇā** | Pointing by dropping the whole meaning | Exclusive implication ("a village on the Ganges" = on the bank) — rejected for *tat tvam asi* | Day 16 |
| **Jīva** | The individual self | Consciousness associated with one mind–body (*vyaṣṭi*), deceived by its own superimposition | Day 15 |
| **Jīvanmukti** | Liberation while living | Freedom recognised and enjoyed in this life, with the body continuing | Day 24 |
| **Jñānī** | The knower | The fourth and highest devotee — "my very Self" (Gītā 7.18) | Day 22 |
| **Karma yoga** | Doing your work as an offering | Action with *Īśvara-arpaṇa-buddhi* and *prasāda-buddhi*, for *citta-śuddhi*; prepares for knowledge, does not liberate | Day 21 |
| **Kartṛtva–bhoktṛtva** | "I am the doer" / "I am the enjoyer" | Superimposition of agency and experience on the Self; what knowledge removes | Day 37 |
| **Kataka** | The clearing nut | Seed that settles mud in water and settles with it; image for knowledge that subsides after removing ignorance (*Ātmabodha*) | Day 31 |
| **Kevala / Sahaja (samādhi)** | Temporary absorption / the natural state | *Kevala*: the ego subsides but returns; *sahaja*: the ego doesn't rise as owner even in activity (Ramana) | Day 26 |
| **Kośa** | Sheath | A covering that takes the Self's shape but is other than it; five listed in Taittirīya 2 | Day 7 |
| **Lakṣaṇa** | An indicator that points rather than describes | A mark distinguishing its target from everything else; how scripture conveys Brahman | Day 11 |
| **Lakṣyārtha** | The meaning pointed to | The implied meaning of a word after its literal meaning is set aside — for *tat* and *tvam*: pure consciousness | Day 16 |
| **Mahāvākya** | Great sentence | The four identity statements: *prajñānaṃ brahma*, *tat tvam asi*, *ayam ātmā brahma*, *aham brahmāsmi* | Day 16 |
| **Manana** | Reflecting | Reasoning through doubts about the teaching; removes *saṃśaya* | Day 20 |
| **Manomaya kośa** | The mental sheath | The sheath "made of mind": thoughts, emotions, sense-perceptions (Taittirīya 2.3) | Day 7 |
| **Mano-nāśa** | Quietening of the mind | "Destruction of mind": the end of its compulsive activity, not of thinking (*Jīvanmukti-viveka*) | Day 24 |
| **Māyā** | The power by which the one appears as many | Īśvara's power producing the world-appearance; itself *mithyā*, so not a second reality | Day 15 |
| **Mithyā** | Dependently real | *Sad-asad-vilakṣaṇa*: neither real (no being of its own, can be sublated) nor non-existent (experienced, functional) | Day 13 |
| **Mokṣa** | Freedom | The fourth *puruṣārtha*; not produced by action — the end of ignorance about what one already is | Day 1 |
| **Mumukṣutva** | Burning desire for freedom | The fourth qualification; the desire for *mokṣa* as the dominant desire | Day 3 |
| **Neti neti** | Not this, not this | Bṛh. 2.3.6: the Self taught by negating everything objectifiable; removes superimposition, not the substrate | Day 17 |
| **Nididhyāsana** | Dwelling | Sustained contemplation of the teaching's meaning; removes *viparīta-bhāvanā* | Day 20 |
| **Nitya-anitya-vastu-viveka** | Telling the lasting from the passing | Discrimination between the eternal (Self) and the non-eternal (everything produced) | Day 3 |
| **Observer is the observed** | The judge is part of what it judges | Krishnamurti: the centre that watches and tries to change thought is itself thought, made of the past | Day 28 |
| **Padārthābhāvanā** | Non-perception of things (stage 6) | Sixth *bhūmikā*: objects cease to present themselves as independent realities | Day 38 |
| **Pañcakośa-viveka** | Discriminating the five sheaths | Separating the Self from body, vitality, mind, intellect and bliss (Pañcadaśī ch. 3) | Day 7 |
| **Parabrahman** | What is prior to "I am" | Nisargadatta: that which knows the "I am" appearing and disappearing; maps to *turīya* | Day 27 |
| **Pāramārthika** | Absolutely real | The order never sublated: Brahman | Day 13 |
| **Pariṇāma** | Real transformation | Change in which the cause is really altered (milk → curd) | Day 15 |
| **Parokṣa jñāna** | Knowing *that* something exists, but as remote | Indirect knowledge — "the tenth man exists (somewhere)" | Day 4 |
| **Perennial idealism** | Consciousness as the ground | Albahari's (2019) name for the contemplative view that an unconditioned consciousness grounds conditioned minds and matter | Day 32 |
| **Phala-vyāpti** | Being lit by the thought | The object illumined by consciousness reflected in the *vṛtti*; not needed for self-luminous Brahman | Day 31 |
| **Prājña** | The deep sleeper | The Self as experiencer of deep sleep — unified, a mass of consciousness, enjoyer of bliss (Māṇḍūkya 5) | Day 8 |
| **Prakaraṇa-grantha** | Topic manual | A short introductory text systematising the canon's vocabulary (*Tattva Bodha*, *Dṛg-Dṛśya-Viveka*, *Pañcadaśī*) | Day 38 |
| **Pramāṇa** | A means of knowing | A valid instrument of knowledge: perception, inference, word (*śabda*), etc. — each with its own domain | Day 4 |
| **Prāṇamaya kośa** | The vital sheath | The sheath "made of vital air": breath, energy, physiological functions (Taittirīya 2.2) | Day 7 |
| **Prapañcopaśama** | Where the world comes to rest | Māṇḍūkya 7's description of *turīya*: that in which the manifold appearance subsides | Day 10 |
| **Prārabdha karma** | Karma already in motion — the arrow in flight | Karma that produced this body-mind; runs until the body falls (BS 4.1.15) | Day 37 |
| **Prasāda-buddhi** | Receiving results as a gift | Accepting whatever result comes as given by the total order | Day 21 |
| **Prasāda (clarity)** | Clarity of a mind free of attraction and aversion | Gītā 2.64–65; in it "all sorrows end"; different from *prasāda-buddhi* (Day 21) | Day 36 |
| **Prasthāna-traya** | The three canonical sources | Upaniṣads (*śruti*), Bhagavad Gītā (*smṛti*), Brahma Sūtra (*nyāya*) | Day 38 |
| **Prātibhāsika** | Merely apparent | The order of dreams, rope-snakes, mirages; sublated by waking or simple inspection | Day 13 |
| **Preyas** | The pleasant | Ends that please the finite person: wealth, pleasure, even heaven (Kaṭha 1.2.1) | Day 2 |
| **Psychological time** | The time of "becoming" | Krishnamurti/Bohm: movement from "what I am" to "what I should be"; the root of inner conflict | Day 28 |
| **Puccha pratiṣṭhā** | The tail, the support | Taittirīya 2.5's image for Brahman as the support of the bliss sheath — not itself a sheath | Day 7 |
| **Pūrṇatva** | Fullness, lacking nothing | The state sought behind every desire; ultimately one's own nature | Day 1 |

| **Pūrvapakṣa** | The objector's view | The opposing position stated in full before it is answered | Day 38 |

| **Rasa** | The lingering taste for objects | The craving that outlasts abstaining; removed only "on seeing the highest" (Gītā 2.59) | Day 36 |
| **Śabda-pramāṇa** | Knowledge through words | Testimony — here, the Upaniṣad as the means of knowledge for the Self | Day 4 |

| **Śama** | Calm of mind | Mastery of the mind's reactions | Day 3 |
| **Samādhāna** | Focus | Settledness of the mind on the goal | Day 3 |
| **Śamādi-ṣaṭka-sampatti** | The six disciplines | *Śama, dama, uparati, titikṣā, śraddhā, samādhāna* | Day 3 |

| **Saṃśaya** | Doubt | Doubt about the means of knowledge or its object; removed by *manana* | Day 20 |
